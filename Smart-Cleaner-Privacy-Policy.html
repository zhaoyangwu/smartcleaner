Smart Cleaner is provided by GenieLampApp ("GenieLampApp," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Cleaner (the "App") accesses and handles information when you use its device storage, media cleanup, app management, notification cleanup, device information, and flashlight features.

This Policy describes Smart Cleaner version 1.2.17 (versionCode 26), package name com.smartclean.storage.junk.aicleaner, as reviewed on the date above.

1 Privacy Summary for the Current Release

Smart Cleaner 1.2.17 performs its scans and processing on your device.

  • INTERNET supports enabled Google Analytics, Google Mobile Ads, privacy-choice updates, and Google Play Billing. ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE supports connection handling and the device-information screen. Google SDK components may merge WAKE_LOCK for reliable background work. Smart Cleaner does not request location permission for these services. The advertising SDK may use the Android Advertising ID where available and permitted.
  • Smart Cleaner uses Google Mobile Ads SDK for app-open, native, and interstitial ads. Google may process the IP address, approximate location derived from it, device or advertising identifiers, diagnostics, and ad interactions to provide, measure, and protect ads. Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before eligible ad requests. Turning usage analytics off does not turn ads off. Smart Cleaner does not send file contents, photos, videos, notification text, or the installed-app list to the advertising SDK.
  • Usage analytics is enabled by default after this Privacy Policy is accepted. Users who accepted an earlier policy also have it enabled after updating to this version. You can turn it off in Settings. Google Analytics receives an SDK-generated app instance identifier, interaction and session data, device and app technical information, and an approximate region derived from the IP address, without location permission. Smart Cleaner also sends fixed events for feature pages, scan results, action confirmations, and cleanup results, including counts, status, and duration. Analytics is not fully anonymous. It does not receive file contents, names, paths, hashes, photos, videos, notification content, your installed-app list, or cross-app usage details. Local cleanup remains available when analytics is turned off. Events also record entry locations, feature clicks, permission-flow states, and aggregate sizes of confirmed file operations. Permanently deleted bytes and bytes moved to trash are reported separately; neither is a measurement of freed disk space.
  • The App does not sell personal or sensitive user data.
  • A file is removed only after you select or confirm a cleanup action. An app is uninstalled only through Android's system confirmation interface.

2 Information Accessed and Processed on Your Device

Files Photos and Videos

When you start a scan or open a cleanup feature, Smart Cleaner may access user-accessible shared storage and authorized media to:

  • Read technical attributes such as file size, file type or format, available creation or modification time, storage category or location, and video duration;
  • Find temporary files, manageable cache files, redundant APK files, large files, screenshots, possible duplicate photos, and large videos;
  • Organize and display cleanup candidates for your review; and
  • Remove only the items you select and confirm.

Scanning, classification, and duplicate checks happen locally. Exact duplicate photo checks compare file sizes, calculate SHA-256 hashes, and compare file bytes to identify fully identical files. Similar-photo checks locally decode bounded, reduced-size samples and compare visual features to group photos that may look alike, including screenshots. Similar-photo candidates can have important differences and are not treated as identical. Smart Cleaner does not upload images for these checks, perform OCR or face, object, or subject recognition, listen to audio, or perform server-side content analysis.

To display a thumbnail or preview that helps you review an item, Android or Smart Cleaner may locally decode enough of that photo or video to present it on your screen. This display-only access stays on the device and is not an upload. Exact duplicate results identify files with matching bytes. Similar-photo groups are review candidates only: no photo is selected by default, you should preview photos before choosing copies to remove, and you must keep at least one photo in each group.

Smart Cleaner respects Android storage restrictions and does not use storage permissions to access another app's private internal storage.

Installed Apps and App Usage Statistics

For app management features, Smart Cleaner may locally process information made available by Android about apps visible to it, including an app's name, package name, icon, storage or cache usage, last-used time, and Wi-Fi or mobile-data usage statistics.

This information is used only to display app-management information and help you choose whether to open Android's uninstall flow. Smart Cleaner does not read the private files of other apps, build advertising profiles, or silently uninstall apps.

Current Notifications

If you enable Notification Access in Android settings, Smart Cleaner may read the title, text, and source app of active notifications so it can display them for review. If you select an eligible notification for removal, the App asks Android to dismiss it.

Smart Cleaner does not reply to messages, does not retain a history of notification text, and does not upload notification information.

Device Storage and Network State

Smart Cleaner may locally read total, used, and available storage, the current active network state, and local network information for display in the device-information screen. ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE does not give the App internet connectivity, and this information is not uploaded.

Local Preferences and Recovery State

The App may store feature preferences and limited operation-recovery state locally on your device so that settings and an interrupted user action can be restored. These local records do not contain uploaded copies of your photos, videos, files, notification text, or installed-app data.

3 Permissions and Special Access

Smart Cleaner may offer a permission setup guide on first launch and remind you about missing access when you open the App again. It also requests relevant access when you use a feature that requires it. You can grant, decline or skip an item and continue using features that do not require it. The exact prompt and available scope depend on your Android version and device manufacturer.

All Files Access

On Android 11 and later, this special access supports Smart Cleaner's core storage-cleanup function by scanning user-accessible shared folders for temporary files, manageable cache files, redundant APK files, and large non-media files. It is used when MediaStore or a folder-by-folder Storage Access Framework selection cannot provide the device-wide cleanup view requested by the user. It does not bypass Android restrictions or permit access to another app's private internal storage.

Legacy Storage Access

On older Android versions, these permissions support reading eligible shared files and deleting user-selected shared files. READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE is limited to Android API 32 and below, and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE is limited to Android API 29 and below.

Photos and Videos

On Android 13 and later, these permissions support user-initiated screenshot, possible duplicate-photo, image-preview, video-cleanup, thumbnail, duration, and preview features across the media scope you authorize. If you grant access only to selected photos and videos on Android 14 or later, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED limits the App to that selection.

Usage Access

If enabled in Android settings, Usage Access allows the App to read the system-provided app storage, cache, last-used, and network-usage statistics described above. It does not allow Smart Cleaner to read another app's private content.

Installed App Visibility

On supported devices, Smart Cleaner may use a manufacturer-specific installed-app permission, such as Xiaomi's com.android.permission.GET_INSTALLED_APPS, together with Android package visibility declarations, to show apps visible to Android. The current release does not request QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES.

App Uninstall Requests

This permission allows Smart Cleaner to open Android's uninstall confirmation screen for an app you select. Smart Cleaner cannot silently uninstall an app.

Notification Access and Notification Permission

Notification Access is special access that you enable separately in Android settings. It allows the current-notification review and dismissal functions described above. POST_NOTIFICATIONS is different: it allows Smart Cleaner to place local tool shortcuts in the notification shade and does not allow the App to read other apps' notifications. The current release does not use remote push notifications.

Camera Permission

The camera permission is used only to switch the device flashlight on or off through Android's CameraManager. Smart Cleaner does not use this permission to take photos or record video or audio.

Network State

INTERNET supports enabled Google Analytics, Google Mobile Ads, privacy-choice updates, and Google Play Billing. ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE supports connection handling and the device-information screen. Google SDK components may merge WAKE_LOCK for reliable background work. Smart Cleaner does not request location permission for these services. The advertising SDK may use the Android Advertising ID where available and permitted.

Smart Cleaner uses Google Mobile Ads SDK for app-open, native, and interstitial ads. Google may process the IP address, approximate location derived from it, device or advertising identifiers, diagnostics, and ad interactions to provide, measure, and protect ads. Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before eligible ad requests. Turning usage analytics off does not turn ads off. Smart Cleaner does not send file contents, photos, videos, notification text, or the installed-app list to the advertising SDK.

4 User Initiated Actions and External Apps

File Deletion

Smart Cleaner shows cleanup candidates for your review. Files are removed only after you select or confirm the action. Depending on your Android version, the system may display an additional confirmation. Deletion may be permanent, so review selected items and keep backups of important files.

App Removal

When you choose to remove an app, Smart Cleaner opens Android's uninstall confirmation screen. Android, not Smart Cleaner, performs the uninstall after your confirmation.

External File Preview

If you choose to open a supported file in another app, Smart Cleaner may use Android's ACTION_VIEW mechanism and grant that app temporary access to the selected file URI. This action is initiated by you. The receiving app handles the file under its own privacy policy, and Smart Cleaner does not control what that app does after you choose it.

Flashlight and Local Notification Shortcuts

The flashlight is activated only when you use the flashlight control. Local notification-shade shortcuts are generated on the device and are not remote messages.

6 Retention and Deletion

Scan data is processed on the device. Temporary scan results may remain only as needed to display the current results or recover an interrupted operation. Preferences and recovery state remain in the App's local storage until they are replaced, cleared through Android settings, or removed when you uninstall the App. Smart Cleaner does not retain notification-text history.

Turning analytics off stops future events and clears the local analytics cache and app instance identifier. It does not delete data already received by Google. Google retains that data under the project retention settings. Privacy requests may be sent through the developer contact on the Play Store listing; no server-side one-click deletion is promised.

Deleting a file through a cleanup feature changes or removes the original item on your device and may be irreversible.

7 Your Choices and Controls

You can:

  • Decline a permission and continue using features that do not require it;
  • Grant access only to selected photos and videos where Android provides that option;
  • Revoke file, photo, video, camera, or notification permissions in Android settings;
  • Turn off All files access, Usage Access, or Notification Access in Android's special-access settings;
  • Clear Smart Cleaner's local storage or uninstall the App; and
  • Choose whether to confirm any file deletion or app uninstall action.

Turning analytics off stops future events and clears the local analytics cache and app instance identifier. It does not delete data already received by Google. Google retains that data under the project retention settings. Privacy requests may be sent through the developer contact on the Play Store listing; no server-side one-click deletion is promised.

8 Security

Local processing reduces the need to transmit personal and sensitive data. Smart Cleaner relies on Android permissions, app sandboxing, and time-limited URI access for supported external previews. No storage method is completely secure, and the security of locally stored information also depends on your device, operating system, lock-screen protection, backups, and other installed apps.

9 Privacy of Children

Smart Cleaner is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required by applicable law. When analytics is enabled, it may process the data described above. Contact GenieLampApp through the Play Store listing if you have a concern.

We update this policy when features or data use change. Google Play handles subscription payments. Smart Cleaner checks purchases and stores the confirmed status on the device to remove ads. All cleaning tools remain free.
